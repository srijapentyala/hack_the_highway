4 Guardrails

  • NoiseNo call at all. The trace cites the rule and why the text is out of scope.
  • Rumorescalate only. No order, dealer, loaner, lot or customer is touched.
  • Check → sendPlan rehearsed on a private Enterprise first. A rejected call is logged, not retried.
  • One boardCompound events share slots and loaners, so nothing is booked twice.
  • PrecisionOne dealer.notify per dealer listing only its orders. Standard tier gets no loaner. Loaner response caps assignments.
